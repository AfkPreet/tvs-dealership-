    POWER TO

    FREESTYLER

    Liquid-Cooled Oil-Coolant Technology

    The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 features a 23-row radiator for optimized cooling, keeping the high-revving engine at ideal temperatures to deliver peak performance.

    Unique Reverse Inclined DOHC Engine

    At the heart of the Apache The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 is a 312.2 cc engine with a compact layout for optimal mass centralization. Forged aluminium pistons — 5% lighter — contribute to a best-in-class power-to-weight ratio, with tuning that delivers higher peak torque across the rev range.

    Race Tuned Linear Stability Control - Cruise Control

    The Apache The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310's Cruise Control lets you settle into the ride. Maintain a steady speed without throttle or clutch input, so you can cover more miles with less effort.

    6-Speed Gearbox with Bidirectional Quickshifter

    Precision shifts and rapid launches are effortless, thanks to the bottom-mounted shift lever and negative back-rack angle machined in all gears lug to lug. Take the thrill of racing up a gear, reducing shift times to the millisecond.

    Race Tuned Slipper Clutch

    The slipper clutch on the Apache The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 enables aggressive downshifts without rear wheel hop—letting you brake later and corner with precision. The assist function boosts torque-handling while reducing clutch effort for smoother control.

    Throttle-By-Wire

    Pinpoint precision in the grip of your palm. The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 features an advanced intelligent throttle body with a turbulence system and a sharper throttle grip with reverse blip, delivering instant power with every twist.

    Engine Coolant Jacket Optimization

    The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310's cylinder head is engineered for best-in-class heat dissipation, keeping engine temperatures low. The result: greater performance, higher revs.

    GTT-Glide Through Technology

    The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310's Glide Through Technology gets you moving with just a slow release of the clutch — no throttle needed. It eliminates the need to coordinate clutch and throttle inputs, making low-speed riding smooth and stall-free.

    Ride Modes

    With 5 ride modes on offer, the The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 enables you to adapt seamlessly and deliver uncompromised performance across a variety of conditions.

    Urban mode

    Tame the streets with the freestyler. The power delivery is tuned to provide quick bursts of acceleration. The dual-channel ABS is primed for a quick response with a mild pulsation feel.

    Rain mode

    Let rain never interfere with play. The engine in Rain Mode limits the speed to 120km/h but provides linear acceleration for plenty of grunt. The ABS comes into its own in this mode with strong lever pulsation and rear lift protection to prevent aquaplaning.

    Sport mode

    In Sport mode, the engine is calibrated to deliver maximum acceleration across all operating conditions while the race-mapped fuel injection provides consistent delivery of power. The engine also enables smooth deceleration for optimized engine braking.

    Track mode

    Access the complete power of the 312.2 cc powerplant in Sport mode. The ride by wire throttle provides instant delivery power on the straights. With minimal ABS interference, the freestyler can truly test the limits of performance.

    Supermoto mode

    Flair to the freestyler, the all-new Supermoto mode in The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 disengages the rear ABS while maximising the power. It's the perfect recipe to keep you drifting with style.

    Built to Order

    The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 is available in a Built-To-Order format, letting you choose the Freestyler Kit that matches your vibe — so your machine performs with a style that's unmistakably yours.

    • Dynamic Kit
    • Dynamic Pro Kit
    • Color Kit

    This kit packs everything you need to keep your Freestyler nimble, sharp, and ready to play.

    Fully Adjustable Front and Rear Suspension

    Fully adjustable front suspension with preload, compression and rebound damping. Fully adjustable rear suspension with preload and rebound damping.

    Tyre Pressure Monitoring System

    A built-in sensor continuously tracks front and rear tyre pressures, displaying real-time data on the cluster. If levels go above or below the recommended range, you are instantly alerted.

    Brass Coated Chain

    The distinctive brass-coated chain enhances performance with its rust-resistant build, while adding standout style.

    If you're ready to push the Freestyler to the limit, this kit enhances the The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310's handling — giving you the confidence to ride harder, yet safer.

    Race Tuned Dynamic Stability Control

    The first-in-class technology optimises braking even while cornering via an advanced system that uses a 6D inertial measurement unit. It adjusts braking and power input to assist riders during critical lowsiding situations and counters righting up when braking in bends.

    Cornering ABS

    The ABS system kicks in and allows you to brake confidently even when you are leaned into the corner.

    Launch Control

    A race-inspired system that lets you accelerate from a standstill with maximum power and zero wheelspin for flawless, explosive starts.

    Drag Torque Control

    Reduces rear-wheel lock during aggressive downshifts—ensuring smoother deceleration and sharper control, even at high speeds.

    Cornering Traction Control

    While throttling out of a corner, the system regulates the torque from the engine to prevent wheel spin.

    Wheelie Control

    Keep the front wheel firmly on the track. The Wheelie Control on the The ALL NEW TVS APACHE RTR 310 calculates the torque request depending on pitch rate, pitch angle, and wheel acceleration to prevent wheel lift.

    Slope Dependent Control

    The system estimates the slope angle and consideration of the axle weight change to enable a proper brake force distribution for two-wheelers.

    Rear Wheel Lift-off Protection

    During intense braking, the system keeps the rear wheel on the ground and significantly reduces the risk of a rollover

    Cornering Cruise Control

    When cornering, speed is automatically reduced by the system to ensure a comfortable lean angle, and the most stable possible ride. After exiting the corner the speed climbs back up to the initial speed set by the cruise control.
