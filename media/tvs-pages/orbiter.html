    Got a Question?

    Here are some frequently asked ones

    How do I purchase the vehicle?
    You can book and purchase the vehicle on the website or by visiting our dealerships. Post successful booking, our Relationship Manager will contact you to take you through the complete purchase process.
    Is it mandatory to book the vehicle, and purchase it? Can't I directly purchase the vehicle at TVS dealerships or online?
    Yes, you must make a booking to purchase the TVS Orbiter. The booking amount is only ₹ 5000* and is adjusted at time of full payment.
    How do I check my order status?
    After you book your vehicle, you will get a link that helps you track the status of your order by logging into the website.
    I would like to buy more than one scooter from my booking registration, is it possible?
    You must book individual vehicles separately. Also, you may book as many vehicles as you like.
