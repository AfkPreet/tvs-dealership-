    • Range

      A real world range of 118+/-5 km can be achieved in standard riding conditions.

      Standard conditions include:

      • A solo rider of average weight (70-80 kg)
      • Riding in Xtealth mode on flat city roads without any additional payload, in a continuous or single-day ride.
      • Recommended tyre pressure maintained (cold tyre pressure: 24 PSI for the front and 32 PSI for the rear).
      • Minimal excessive simultaneous use of brakes and accelerator.
      • This does not account for sleep current, where power consumption averages 3-4% over the first 16 hours, even when the vehicle is in the off condition.

      Frequently Asked Questions
      I don’t live in a city where the TVS X is currently sold, but I’d really like to buy the vehicle and take it to my city. How can I do that?
      Buying the TVS X from a city where it is currently not sold and taking it to another city may be a challenge due to the state legalities. Also, we do not have an after-sale service for TVS X in such cities, which may hinder your experience.
      How do I purchase the vehicle?
      You can book and purchase the vehicle on the website. Our Relationship Manager will then contact you to take you through the process.
      Is it mandatory to book the vehicle, and purchase it? Can’t I directly purchase the vehicle at TVS dealerships or online?
      Yes, you must make a booking to purchase the TVS X. The booking amount is only ₹ 5000.
      How do I check my order status?
      After you book your vehicle, you will get a link that helps you track the status of your order by logging into the website.
