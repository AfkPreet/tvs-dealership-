    • EFI system ET – FI Eco Thrust Fuel Injection Technology

    • Bore (mm) 53.5
    • Stroke (mm) 48.8
    • Swept Volume (cc) 109.7
    • Compression Ratio (-) 10.0 : 1
    • Max speed kmph 90 Kmph
    • Air Filter Paper Filter Element
    • Emission Compliance BS VI

    Brakes

    • Front (Type / Dia) (mm) Drum: 130 | Disc: 240
    • Rear (Type / Dia) (mm) Drum: 110 (Synchro)

    Electricals

    • Battery 12V 4Ah MF
    • Headlamp (W) LED, 11W
    • Horn Type / No 12V DC / 1
    • Ignition Type ECU
    • Tail Lamp / Stop Lamp (W) 5 / 10

    Suspension

    • Front Telescopic (Oil damped)
    • Rear 5 step adjustable Hydraulic Shock Absorber

    Transmission

    • Clutch type Wet, Multiple - Disc
    • Gear Shift Pattern All up
    • No of Gears 4 Speed Constant Mesh

    Tyre

    • Front 2.75 X 17 41P 4PR, Tubeless
    • Rear 3.0 X 17 50P 6PR, Tubeless

    Capacities

    • Fuel Tank (Litres) 10
    • Engine oil (litres) 1

    Dimension

    • Length (mm) 1984
    • Width @ Handlebar (mm) 750
    • Height (mm) 1080
    • Wheel base (mm) 1260
    • Ground Clearance (mm) 172
    • Kerb Weight (kg) 115 (Drum), 116 (Disc)

          What is the engine capacity of TVS Star City Plus?

          The engine capacity of the TVS Star City Plus is 109.7cc. It is a single-cylinder, 4-stroke, air-cooled engine that features TVS's Eco-Thrust Fuel Injection (ET-Fi) technology.

          Does TVS Star City Plus come with LED headlamp or DRLs?

          Yes, the TVS Star City Plus comes equipped with a segment-first LED headlamp with integrated DRL.

          Does Star City Plus have a digital or analog meter?

          The Star City Plus features a semi-digital instrument cluster, which typically includes an analogue speedometer and a Digital display for the odometer and fuel gauge.

          What is the engine power of TVS Star City Plus?

          The TVS Star City Plus has a maximum power output of 8.08 bhp at 7,350 rpm. It is powered by a 109.7cc single-cylinder, 4-stroke, air-cooled engine.

          What is the torque output of TVS Star City Plus?

          The maximum torque output of TVS Star City Plus is 8.7 Nm at 4500 rpm.

          What is the type of engine used in the TVS Star City Plus?

          It uses a single-cylinder, 4-Stroke, Air-Cooled engine with Eco Thrust Fuel Injection (ET-Fi) technology.

          How many gears does TVS Star City Plus have?

          The TVS Star City Plus has a 4-speed manual gearbox.

          Is TVS Star City Plus easy to maintain?

          Yes, it is widely considered an easy-to-maintain commuter motorcycle due to its simple and reliable engine architecture.

          What is the typical service cost for TVS Star City Plus?

          The service cost is generally low and affordable, with the first five services being free (excluding consumables like engine oil). Overall running costs are very budget-friendly.

          What is the service interval for TVS Star City Plus?

          The initial service intervals are typically: -

          • 1st Service at 500-750km or 30 Days
          • 2nd at 2500-3000km or 3rd month
          • 3rd at 5000-6000 km or 6th month
          • 4th service at 8500-9000km or 9th month
          • 5th service at 11500km -12000km or 12th month
          • After free service – the service schedule is at every 3000 Kms or 3rd months

          What warranty does TVS provide on Star City Plus?

          TVS generally provides a warranty of 5 years or 60,000km, whichever comes first.

          Is TVS Star City Plus good for daily commuting?

          Yes, it is an excellent choice for daily commuting, highly regarded for its fuel efficiency, smooth ride quality, and comfort in city traffic.

          Can TVS Star City Plus handle rural or uneven roads well?

          Yes, with approximately 172 mm of ground clearance and a robust suspension setup (telescopic front, 5-step adjustable hydraulic rear), it can handle rough, uneven roads quite well.

          Is TVS Star City Plus suitable for long-distance rides?

          Yes, the TVS Star City Plus is suitable for long-distance rides due to its comfort features and reliable engine.
